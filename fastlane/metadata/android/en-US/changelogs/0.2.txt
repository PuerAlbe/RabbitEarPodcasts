Initial commit

Created dual syndication schedule - start at the begginning and work your way forward, or start with the latest and work backward with scheduled injections of the newest episodes on release.

Auto download 1 episode from each podcast upon tapping a Syndicated button. This covers long trips which may include dead spots.

The play button saves current episode play status, and your subscriptions and played episode states are all saved every 15 minutes. Manual buttons provided for migration to new devices.

No ads, telemetry, IAPs, or begging for cash. This app is a tool, it does its job and stays the f*%& out of the way.
